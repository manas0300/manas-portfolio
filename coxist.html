COXIST AI

Freelance Branding / Visual Identity System for an AI-Powered Gen Z Learning Platform

Project Type Freelance Client Engagement
My Role Brand Identity Designer
Domain / Target EdTech · AI Learning · Gen Z (US Market)
Timeline 2024

01 — Overview

COXIST is an AI-powered learning platform tailored for Gen Z students in the US EdTech space—engineered to be a one-stop digital ecosystem unifying coursework synthesis, intelligent tutoring, research workflows, and collaborative productivity.

The Challenge

Beyond Cold Tech Tropes

Most educational software feels rigid, institutional, or overly mechanical. The branding objective was to differentiate COXIST by replacing clinical AI aesthetics with an identity that feels modern, accessible, and intellectually stimulating—balancing cutting-edge artificial intelligence with genuine human empathy.

Visual Direction

Minimal · Futuristic · Approachable

Defined by high-contrast obsidian backgrounds, luminous sunrise gradients, razor-sharp geometric precision, and clean typographic rhythms. The resulting visual language radiates youthful energy without compromising on technological sophistication.

My Freelance Role

End-to-End Identity Architecture

As the sole freelance brand designer, I led the complete visual creation process: discovery sessions, concept sketches, logomark vector drafting in Adobe Illustrator, color theory formulation, typographic hierarchy, multi-scale export packaging, and high-fidelity contextual mockups.

Audience Alignment

Gen Z Student Ecosystem

Designed specifically for digital natives in American colleges and universities. Every visual touchpoint was calibrated to feel native across mobile OS widgets, tablet note-taking apps, campus billboards, and high-focus desktop study sessions.

02 — Brand Identity System

The name coXist captures the core philosophy: the seamless coexistence between human learning and artificial intelligence. The custom symbol merges the organic curvature of a human interface (the enclosing ‘C’) with a central nexus and convergence point (the interlocking ‘X’). It represents balance, partnership, and empowerment rather than AI replacement.

03 — Brand Applications

Demonstrating the brand system across key physical and digital touchpoints—from urban campus billboards to app store presence and executive stationery.

01 · Desktop Workspace

Desktop Learning Environment · MacBook Pro

High-focus desktop interface where students engage with the AI copilot, manage multi-subject workspaces, and review synthesized research. The dark UI reduces visual fatigue during extended nocturnal study sessions.

02 · Out of Home (OOH)

Urban Campus Billboard Campaign

Large-scale out-of-home awareness placement designed for university transit corridors and student hubs. High-contrast typography and glowing gradient accents capture attention instantly from distance.

03 · Mobile Touchpoint

Mobile App Icon · iOS & Android

Iconic, high-legibility app launcher icon crafted for small screen boundaries. The warm radiant mark stands out distinctly against dark wallpapers and cluttered grid menus.

04 · Tablet Experience

Tablet Interface · Apple iPad

Responsive tablet layout tailored for active lecture note-taking, stylus annotation, and split-screen research alongside textbook PDFs.

05 · Architectural Branding

Illuminated Exterior Signage

Fabricated physical logomark with internal edge lighting for student lounge installations, startup incubator headquarters, and tech campus wayfinding.

06 · Physical Collateral

Business Card & Identity Stationery

Matte dark soft-touch business cards featuring precision spot UV gloss over the gradient logomark, providing a tactile, premium physical extension of the brand.

07 · Ecosystem Overview

Unified Multi-Surface Identity Ecosystem

Harmonized brand application across mobile, tablet, desktop, outdoor, and stationery touchpoints—demonstrating consistent visual rhythm and brand integrity at every scale.
