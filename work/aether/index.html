Role & Scope

I worked across Aether's visual communication and event collateral, including merchandise, wristbands, lanyards, ID cards, fest banners, social posts, and related promotional material — extending the visual identity consistently across both digital and physical touchpoints.

As Design Head for the 2026 edition, I led the overarching aesthetic direction for Mahindra University's annual cultural festival, establishing a high-energy design language characterized by distressed custom typography, high-contrast palettes, and youth-culture streetwear references.

02 / Identity System

Core Brandmark & Event Communication

The brand identity is anchored by a dynamic typographic vortex — distressed letterforms spiraling inwards in alternating vermilion red and stark white against pure black. Event communication posters integrated retro-pop floral motifs and custom badges to announce the 7-day festival timeline.
