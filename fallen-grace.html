Overview

Fallen Grace is a collaborative 3D animated short film created with a friend as part of coursework at Mahindra University, developed and rendered in Unreal Engine. The film tells the dark, mythic tale of Yamen, a resurrected warrior augmented in cybernetic armor, embarking on an unyielding quest for divine retribution following the murder of his demigod wife.

Advancing across a bleak ash-strewn battlefield and traversing a monumental bridge of blood and bone, Yamen confronts a colossal blood-red Torii gate portal into the celestial realm. There, he faces the governing pantheon known as The Three Heads—Perdit (the God of Death), Seraph (the Creator), and Darius (the All Protector)—setting the stage for an epic standoff between mortal vengeance and celestial dominion.
