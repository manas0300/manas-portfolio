A little about me.

I'm Manas — a Computation & Media student at Mahindra University with a deep interest in where technology and creative expression intersect.

My work spans filmmaking, 3D environments, VFX, motion graphics, interactive media and brand design. I enjoy the process of building things — whether that's crafting a visual narrative, constructing a 3D world, developing a game prototype, or shaping a brand identity.

I work with tools like Unreal Engine, Unity, Blender, Adobe Creative Suite, Figma, Framer and Canva — alongside programming in Python and C. What drives me is the challenge of combining creative thinking with technical execution.
