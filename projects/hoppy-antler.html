Hoppy Antler

Category

Game Design / 3D / Interactive

Year

2024

Role

Game Designer · 3D Generalist

Overview

Created as part of Mobile & Cloud Game Design coursework at Mahindra University, Hoppy Antler is a 3D road-crossing survival game featuring an adventurous young deer journeying beyond the quiet shelter of the forest into the expansive, fast-moving world beyond.

Players guide the deer across multi-lane highways populated by speeding trucks, delivery vans, and dense traffic corridors. The project investigates arcade hop timing, responsive mobile controls, low-poly environmental worldbuilding, and visual narrative development.

Core Systems & Design

01 — SURVIVAL MECHANICS

Arcade Hop Timing

Implemented precision hop-and-dodge locomotion where players time movements between traffic flows, balancing risk and reward to maximize score.

02 — 3D ENVIRONMENT

Low-Poly Worldbuilding

Designed modular low-poly assets in Blender—including roadside pine trees, asphalt lanes, and stylized vehicular obstacles with crisp vertex lighting.

03 — VISUAL STORYTELLING

Narrative Comic Rollout

Crafted an episodic introductory comic strip establishing the deer's motivation to cross the highway barrier and discover new horizons.

04 — INTERACTION DESIGN

Mobile & Cloud Controls

Configured responsive touch and keyboard inputs tailored for casual, accessible gameplay sessions across mobile devices and cloud platforms.
