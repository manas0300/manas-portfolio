Campus Crave — Mobile UX Case Study

Campus Crave

Category

UI/UX / Product Design

Year

2025

Overview

Mobile application design and user experience framework created to streamline campus dining, order management, and community interactions for university students.

Process & Contribution

Conducted user interviews with students to identify friction points in campus dining rush hours. Formulated end-to-end user journeys, low-fidelity wireframes, interactive Figma prototypes, and an accessible mobile design system featuring streamlined menu discovery, customized dietary filters, and live order tracking.
